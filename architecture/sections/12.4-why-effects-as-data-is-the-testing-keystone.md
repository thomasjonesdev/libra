# 12.4 Why effects-as-data is the testing keystone

## In this vault

- Parent: [[12-the-effects-runtime]]

---

A React test of "click connect → fetch → persist → re-render" needs a mock server, mocked storage, and async test plumbing. The Libra equivalent (an integration test, [[15.4-integration-tests-full-flows-no-mocks|§15.4]]) is synchronous and mock-free: click Connect, assert the intercepted `Effect::Http` describes the right request, feed an `HttpResult` fixture to its continuation, assert the state and the follow-on `Effect::KvSet`, and assert the rebuilt view shows Live. Every branch — timeout, 500, malformed body — is one more fixture, zero more infrastructure.
