# 5.6 Time Travel & Replay

## In this vault

- Parent: [[05-state-and-actions]]

---

Because the reducer is pure and `Action` is serializable, the runtime can record `(action, state_hash)` per step at negligible cost. Replaying an action log against `init()` reproduces the session exactly. This is not a feature built later; it falls out of [[05.3-purity-rules|§5.3]] and is wired into the inspector ([[18.5-time-travel-capture|§18.5]]) and the test harness ([[15.6-replay-tests|§15.6]]) from day one.
