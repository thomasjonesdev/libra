# 6.6 Parser

## In this vault

- Parent: [[06-view-and-markup]]

## In code

- `crates/libra-markup/src/lib.rs` — parse `.lx` → AST with spans

---

Hand-rolled recursive-descent in `libra-markup` (no parser-generator dependency): a JSX-shaped grammar is small, and owning the parser means owning error quality. Spans on every node. The parser is also consumed by the LSP ([[18.6-editor-tooling|§18.6]]) and the formatter (`libra fmt`).
